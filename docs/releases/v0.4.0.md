# v0.4.0 - Configurable Open-Trend Discovery

This release moves the workbench from a fixed-watchlist collector toward a configurable daily trend-discovery system.

## Highlights

- Maintain the default 69-person KOL list from the dashboard with search, platform/category filters, pagination, add, edit, disable, and delete actions.
- Maintain Lark/Feishu push targets as non-secret metadata. The dashboard stores environment-variable names only; real webhook and signing-secret values remain local.
- Manage RSS/Atom subscriptions from the dashboard and collect each enabled feed into its own evidence artifact and trace run.
- Run four open-trend lanes before fixed watchlists: global AI, visual/multimodal models, AI x Web3, and AI x finance.
- Add an independent frontier visual-model radar for Black Forest Labs, Runway, Luma, and Kling.
- Upgrade to digest quality version 6 and research trace schema version 3.
- Enrich bot cards with featured new signals, fuller summaries, and why-it-matters context.

## Why

The previous workflow spent most of its research budget on fixed domestic-lab sweeps. That made the trace look busy while leaving too little open discovery for same-day industry conversations and releases outside the watchlist. A concrete example was FLUX 3: Black Forest Labs was outside the domestic-lab matrix, and there was no independent visual-model lane.

The new workflow learns the day's entities, debates, and authors first, then uses maintained KOL and lab lists for verification and completion.

## Quality Contract

- Twelve mandatory query groups: the four open-trend lanes plus community, quota/access, Chinese frontier models, X viewpoints, three domestic-lab tracks, and dynamic KOL discovery.
- Attributable candidates require a deep URL, title, in-window publication date, and a meaningful excerpt.
- At least three selected items must originate from trend discovery and cover at least two lanes.
- Dynamic KOL discovery requires at least ten candidates, three topic-expansion selections, and two selected authors outside the maintained watchlist.
- Every hot topic records its trend lane, why it matters now, and the real debate or boundary.

## Compatibility

- Existing quality v5 digests remain readable and valid in compatibility mode.
- `config/kol.yaml` and `config/push.yaml` remain as legacy references, while the effective dashboard configuration takes precedence.
- The static dashboard still works by opening `index.html` directly; no local server is required.

## Validation

- 13 Python regression tests.
- Python compilation, JavaScript syntax, and YAML parse checks.
- Playwright coverage for configuration persistence, KOL/push/RSS CRUD, KOL pagination, secret-like input rejection, and mobile overflow.
- Lark dry-run resolves exactly one primary target under `primary_only`; no real webhook is included in the public repository.
